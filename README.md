#  CTF Writeups - Null-Trace0

Welcome to my CTF (Capture The Flag) writeups repository. 

This repo serves as my personal knowledge base and portfolio for the CTFs, TryHackMe rooms, and HackTheBox machines I complete. I believe that documenting the "how" and "why" of solving a challenge is the best way to solidify my understanding and share knowledge with the community.

--- 

## Methodology
For every writeup, I focus on:

Reconnaissance: What information was available?

Exploitation: The exact steps and payloads used.

Remediation: How the vulnerability should have been fixed (Defensive mindset).
