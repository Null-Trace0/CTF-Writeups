#  CTF Writeups 
---
Welcome to my CTF (Capture The Flag) writeups repository. 

This repo serves as my personal knowledge base and portfolio for the CTF events and challenges I complete. I believe that documenting the "how" and "why" of solving a challenge is the best way to solidify my understanding, build a hacker's mindset, and share knowledge with the community.

###  Focus Areas
While I enjoy all aspects of offensive security, my primary interests in CTFs are:
- **Reverse Engineering & Binary Exploitation (Pwn)**
- **Web Exploitation**
- **Forensics & Malware Analysis**
- **OSINT & Cryptography**

### Repository Structure
To keep things organized, writeups are split into two main categories:
1. **Official Events:** Time-bound CTF competitions (e.g., picoCTF, NahamCon, HTB Cyber Apocalypse).
2. **Open-Source/Standalone Challenges:** Permanently available practice environments and open-source CTF challenges.
